Privacy Policy & Data Rights

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 | Application: Simply Booking Mobile PMS

Simply Booking ("we", "our", or "us") provides an industry-level Property Management System (PMS) mobile application designed for hotel owners, administrators, and hospitality staff to manage room inventories, reservations, housekeeping turnovers, and invoicing.

This Privacy Policy details how personal, guest, and property information is collected, processed, and protected when using our mobile application and related API services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide professional hotel management services, Simply Booking collects the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials & Profile: User full name, email address, password hash, and property ownership role.
  • Property Details: Hotel name, address, city, country, base operating currency, room categories, and total inventory count.
  • Operational Booking & Guest Records: Guest name, phone number, check-in and check-out timestamps, room assignment, and transaction amounts.
  • Housekeeping & Staff Records: Staff names, customized permission sets (JSONB access controls), and room cleaning turnover logs.
  • Device Identifiers & Push Tokens: Expo Push Notification tokens and device OS version used strictly for dispatching real-time housekeeping and reservation alerts.

Strict Privacy Commitment: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any hotel guest records, contact lists, or financial data to third parties or advertising networks.

2. How We Use and Protect Your Data

We process your data strictly to deliver PMS functionality, including:

  • Authenticating users and enforcing staff access permissions.
  • Preventing room double-bookings via ACID-compliant database locking mechanisms.
  • Calculating room tariffs, folios, tax totals, and generating itemized receipts.
  • Dispatching real-time WebSocket and push alerts when housekeeping turnovers occur.

3. Security & Data Storage

All data transmitted between the Simply Booking mobile application and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3). Database records are stored in managed PostgreSQL instances with continuous backups and strict tenant isolation.

4. Third-Party Integrations

Simply Booking interacts with trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging: For native mobile push notification dispatch.
  • WhatsApp Web / Deep Linking: Allows front-desk staff to initiate booking confirmation messages directly to guest phone numbers upon request.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion Request Portal

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA), all hotel administrators and staff have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account credentials, property configuration, booking archives, and operational data. You do not need to keep the app installed on your device to submit this request.
